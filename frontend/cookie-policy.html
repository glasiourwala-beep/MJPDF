Cookie Policy

This policy explains how MJPDF uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser local storage.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Local storage is a similar browser feature used to remember settings.

Why we use them

We use these technologies to provide essential site functionality and to remember your cookie preferences.

Essential cookies / local storage

Essential storage is used so the site can function and so we can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies. Core PDF tools do not require advertising cookies.

Analytics cookies

MJPDF does not currently load third-party analytics by default. If analytics are added later, this policy and the cookie settings panel will be updated, and optional consent will be requested where required.

Managing cookies

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the cookie banner. You can also change your choice later using “Cookie settings” in the footer where available. You may clear site data through your browser settings.
