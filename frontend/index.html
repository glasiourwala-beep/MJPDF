Online PDF tools for everyday documents

MJPDF is a free online PDF toolkit designed for students, freelancers, and teams who need reliable document workflows without installing desktop software. Upload a file, run the tool on the server, and download the result. Files are processed for your request and cleaned up automatically—nothing is kept permanently on the server.

What you can do with MJPDF

Convert documents to PDF

Turn Microsoft Word (.doc / .docx) and PowerPoint (.ppt / .pptx) files into PDF for easy sharing and printing. Conversion runs server-side so layout stays as close as possible to the original file.

Compress PDF files

Reduce PDF file size with Low, Medium, or High compression so attachments fit email limits and load faster on mobile—while keeping text readable.

Merge, split, extract, and delete pages

Combine multiple PDFs into one file, split a PDF into single-page files, extract only the pages you need, or remove unwanted pages before you share a document.

Images and page tools

Build a PDF from JPG or PNG images, export PDF pages as images, rotate pages, reorder pages, add a text watermark, password-protect a PDF, or unlock a PDF when you know the password.

Why use free online PDF tools?

Browser-based PDF tools save time when you are on a shared computer, a Chromebook, or a phone. MJPDF focuses on a clean dark interface, clear upload progress, and straightforward downloads. No account is required for core tools.

For best results, use original Office files when converting to PDF, and keep individual uploads within the stated size limit. If a file fails, try again with a smaller file or a different format.

Privacy and security

Uploads are handled over HTTPS. Temporary files are stored only as long as needed to finish processing, then removed. Review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details. Cookie preferences can be managed anytime from the footer.
